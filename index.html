    How to be Ben

    Controls

    KeyboardWASD / arrows walk everywhere. Approach a door and press E or Enter to enter. E talks to nearby editor-placed NPCs; inside it otherwise observes. Editors: the build catalog stays visible at the bottom; press / to search, use arrow keys between cards, drag to place, Ctrl+Z/Y to undo/redo, Ctrl+D to duplicate, Delete to remove, and Esc to finish. Shift strolls; Space plays challenges; M toggles sound.
    ControllerModern Xbox, PlayStation, Switch-style, and generic browser gamepads are supported. D-pad / left stick walks · A / Cross confirms or walks to the selected building · X / Square observes · B / Circle closes · shoulders change selection · Menu opens help.
    Touch / MouseClick or tap any walkable ground to move there; click a building to walk to its front door. Manual movement cancels the route. The walking pad, residents, observations, and actions can also be tapped.

    Tip: The highlighted daily Virtue earns bonus practice. There is still no perfect route—Franklin’s ledger was for reflection, not perfection.
